This page walks you through everything you need to go from zero to your first completed task — requesting access, signing in, opening the workspace, and letting Lilac get to work. The whole process takes just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Access">
    Lilac is currently in early access. Go to [lilac.codes/join](https://lilac.codes/join), enter your email address, and join the waitlist. You'll receive a notification as soon as your spot is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Once you have access, go to [lilac.codes/agent](https://lilac.codes/agent). Sign in using one of the available options: **GitHub**, **Google**, or an **email and password** combination. Choose whichever fits your workflow best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Workspace">
    After signing in, you land directly in the agent workspace at `/agent`. This is your home base in Lilac — where you send tasks, watch progress unfold in real time, and review everything Lilac produces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Your First Task">
    Type a plain-language goal into the chat input. You can ask Lilac to build a page, explore a codebase, fix a bug, draft content, or anything else you'd like it to tackle. When you're ready, press **Enter** or click the send button to kick things off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch It Work">
    Lilac begins working immediately. The workspace thread shows each step it takes, the tools it uses, and its progress as it goes. You don't need to stay on the page — runs continue in the background on Lilac's worker, and you can return at any time to check in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Result">
    When Lilac finishes, the output appears in the thread. Read through the steps it took, inspect what it produced, and ask for changes if needed. For tasks connected to a GitHub repository, Lilac opens a pull request so you can review and approve the changes before anything is merged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your task involves GitHub, Notion, or another integration, connect those tools before you start. Giving Lilac access upfront means it has the context it needs from the first message. See [Connect Tools](using/connecting-to-mcps) for step-by-step instructions.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Lilac is in early beta and new capabilities are being added regularly. If something you'd like to do isn't supported yet, check back soon — the feature set is growing quickly.
</Note>
